# Curated Repositories for 500+ Medical Imaging Papers & Datasets

To assist in compiling a literature registry of over 500+ research papers and public clinical datasets, this document provides direct access to curated, open-source repositories tracking medical foundation models, self-supervised learning (SSL) architectures, and segmentation benchmarks.

---

## 1. Curated Repositories (500+ Papers & Datasets)

### Awesome-Self-Supervised-Learning-in-Medical-Imaging
*   **Repository URL:** [Awesome-Self-Supervised-Learning-in-Medical-Imaging](https://github.com/SaeedShurrab/awesome-self-supervised-learning-in-medical-imaging)
*   **Target Scope:** Comprehensive list of self-supervised learning (SSL) medical research papers published since 2017.
*   **Methodology Focus:** Catalogs reconstruction pretext tasks (Masked Autoencoders, Contrastive Pretraining, DINOv3, Barlow Twins) applied to CT, MRI, X-Ray, and Ultrasound scans.

### Awesome-Foundation-Models-in-Medical-Imaging
*   **Repository URL:** [Awesome-Foundation-Models-in-Medical-Imaging](https://github.com/JeremyRJX/Awesome-Foundation-Models-in-Medical-Imaging)
*   **Target Scope:** Dedicated index tracking Generalist Medical AI, large vision-language models (VLMs), and 3D foundation backbones (e.g., VISTA3D, SegVol, Pan-FM).

### Awesome-Medical-Large-Language-Models
*   **Repository URL:** [Awesome-Medical-Large-Language-Models](https://github.com/burglarhobbit/Awesome-Medical-Large-Language-Models)
*   **Target Scope:** A large index focusing on clinical decision support, medical reasoning, report generation, and treatment recommendation models.

### Awesome-Medical-Image-Segmentation-Dataset
*   **Repository URL:** [Awesome-Medical-Image-Segmentation-Dataset](https://github.com/ziyangwang007/Awesome-Medical-Image-Segmentation-Dataset)
*   **Target Scope:** A massive registry of public medical image segmentation datasets.
*   **Modality Scope:** 3D CT, MRI, PET, Ultrasound, and Pathology slides organized by international MICCAI challenges (BraTS, FLARE, KiTS, AMOS, TotalSegmentator).

### Awesome-Foundation-Models-for-Advancing-Healthcare
*   **Repository URL:** [Awesome-Foundation-Models-for-Advancing-Healthcare](https://github.com/jshilong/Awesome-Foundation-Models-for-Advancing-Healthcare)
*   **Target Scope:** Surveys generalist medical AI models, datasets, benchmarks, safety evaluations, and clinical translation guidelines.

## 2. Integrated Clinical Dataset Hubs
These open registries host the raw scans and metadata used to train these models:

| Platform | Modality Types | Target Anatomical Structure | Public Access Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TCIA (Cancer Archive)** | CT, MRI, PET, X-Ray | Whole-body tumor cohorts | [TCIA Homepage](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/) |
| **Zenodo (Open Science)** | Multi-modal volumes | Segmentations (TotalSegmentator) | [Zenodo Search](https://zenodo.org/) |
| **Awesome MedSeg** | 3D Volumetric scans | Abdominal, Thoracic, and Brain organs | [MedSeg Registry](https://github.com/ziyangwang007/Awesome-Medical-Image-Segmentation-Dataset) |
| **MIMIC-CXR Database** | 2D projection | Thoracic cavity, Lungs, Heart | [PhysioNet MIMIC-CXR](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-cxr/2.0.0/) |
